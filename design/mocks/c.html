The standup, without the meeting.

An open-source Slack app for async daily check-ins that you run yourself: your Cloudflare account, your database, up and running in about 15 minutes. A DailyBot alternative you own.

Deploy your own Read the source (MIT)
What it does

Nine small things that replace a meeting.

  1. 01

    Check-ins at the right hour

    At the time you choose, in each person's own timezone, sunup DMs a short form with questions you configure.

  2. 02

    One tidy digest

    At digest time, a single message in the channel summarizes everyone's answers. Late check-ins update it in place.

  3. 03

    Blockers that don't vanish

    Each blocker is tracked until resolved: the next prompt asks "still blocked?", the digest shows its age, and App Home keeps a live board.

  4. 04

    Nudges and vacation mode

    One reminder before the digest goes out, and /sunup snooze 5 when someone is away.

  5. 05

    Kudos

    /kudos @teammate for shipping the thing, with a 30-day leaderboard.

  6. 06

    An optional mood pulse

    A 1-5 question that reports the team average only, and only once three or more people have answered.

  7. 07

    Sundown and the weekly retro

    /sundown is the evening checkout twin of the morning form, and the weekly retro looks back over the week.

  8. 08

    Reports and export

    /sunup report makes a charted, signed report page that lasts 24 hours; /sunup export hands you a CSV.

  9. 09

    It reads nothing it isn't given

    Four scopes only: chat:write, commands, im:write, users:read. No message-reading. It sees what people type into its own forms.

Setup

Deploy it before your coffee cools.

Free-tier Cloudflare Workers, or plain Node. Create the Slack app from the bundled manifest, then:

# deploy
$ git clone https://github.com/briancorbin/sunup && cd sunup
$ npm install
$ npx wrangler login
$ npx wrangler d1 create sunup
$ npm run db:migrate
$ npm run deploy

# secrets
$ npx wrangler secret put SLACK_BOT_TOKEN
$ npx wrangler secret put SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET

# in Slack
/invite @Sunup
/sunup setup
/sunup join

About 15 minutes. Your Slack app, your Cloudflare account, your database.

Async daily check-ins for Slack. Yours to run.

At a time you choose, sunup DMs each teammate a short form in their own timezone, then posts one tidy digest to the channel. Blockers are tracked until someone resolves them. Open source, self-hosted, free. No per-seat pricing, ever.

🔒 No message-reading scopes, ever
Deploy your own in 15 minutes View on GitHub

sunup isn't a hosted service. You run it, on Cloudflare's free tier or plain Node, and your team's data stays in your infrastructure.

What it does

The standup, without the meeting.

☀️Async check-ins

Each participant is prompted at the chosen hour in their own timezone and answers a short form, with questions you configure, whenever suits them.

📰Channel digest

At digest time, one message summarizes everyone's answers in the team channel. Check in late and the digest updates in place to include you.

🚧Blocker tracking

A blocker isn't a one-day mention. The next prompt asks "still blocked?", the digest shows every open blocker with its age, and the App Home has a live board.

⏰Nudges and vacation mode

Non-responders get one friendly reminder before the digest. /sunup snooze 5 pauses your prompts and keeps you out of "waiting on" lists.

🎉Kudos

/kudos @teammate for shipping the thing posts a celebration and feeds a 30-day leaderboard.

📈Mood pulse

An optional 1 to 5 mood question, shown in the digest only as a team average, and only once three or more people have answered.

🌇/sundown

An evening checkout twin: what shipped, what's carrying over, wins worth celebrating. Same engine, sunset tone. A channel can run both.

📆Weekly retro

On the week's last scheduled day: participation by day, mood trend, every blocker from the week, kudos totals and streak leaders.

📊Reports and export

/sunup report mints a 24-hour signed link to a charted, printable team report. /sunup export gives you the full history as CSV.

Privacy by design

It only ever sees what people type into its own forms.

sunup requests no message-reading scopes. It can't see your channels, your DMs or your history. Four scopes, and one of them is just for timezones. Data retention is a single setting; purge anything older than N days automatically.

  • chat:write post prompts and digests
  • commands the /sunup, /sundown and /kudos slash commands
  • im:write open the DM that carries the check-in form
  • users:read timezones, so each person is prompted at their own morning

Setup

Your Slack app, your Cloudflare account, your database. About 15 minutes.

Deploy the Worker

You need a Cloudflare account (the free tier is far more than enough), Node 18 or later, and permission to install a custom app in your Slack workspace.

$ git clone https://github.com/briancorbin/sunup.git && cd sunup
$ npm install && npx wrangler login
# create the database, then paste its id into apps/cloudflare/wrangler.jsonc
$ npx wrangler d1 create sunup --cwd apps/cloudflare
$ npm run db:migrate --workspace apps/cloudflare
$ npm run deploy

Create the Slack app from the manifest

Put your Worker URL into slack-app-manifest.yml, then at api.slack.com/apps choose Create New App → From a manifest and paste it in. Install it to your workspace and grab the Bot User OAuth Token and the Signing Secret.

Wire them together

$ cd apps/cloudflare
$ npx wrangler secret put SLACK_BOT_TOKEN
$ npx wrangler secret put SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET

Use it

In your team channel, invite the app and run setup. Teammates join with one command. /sunup help shows everything else.

/invite @Sunup
/sunup setup      ← creates the check-in with sensible defaults
/sunup join       ← each teammate, once

Runs where you want

The bot is portable. The platform is a plugin.

Cloudflare Workers

The reference deployment: a Worker, a D1 database, a cron trigger. Fits in the free tier.

Plain Node

The same bot as a Node 24 process with node:sqlite storage and zero native dependencies. A Dockerfile and compose file are included.

Somewhere else

An adapter is an HTTP entry, a Storage implementation and a trigger, about 150 lines. Lambda, Deno and Bun ports are welcome.
